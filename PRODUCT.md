# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
High-ticket cosmetic and implant dental patients in India and internationally (dental tourists) seeking premium, trustworthy, and pain-free care.

## Product Purpose
A luxury editorial-style homepage and showcase for FMS Dental clinics, demonstrating digital precision, clinical excellence, and aesthetic craftsmanship.

## Brand Personality
Elegant, Calm, Sophisticated, Tactile, Architectural.

## Anti-references
Neon medical blues, corporate stock photos of smiling doctors pointing at cameras, overexposed bright sterile lighting, cluttered SaaS layouts, generic templates.

## Design Principles
* **Confidence through simplicity:** Remove clutter, only show information that builds clinical trust and makes decisions clear.
* **Tactile materiality:** Leverage natural textures (oak, brass, stone) and editorial grids to evoke premium hospitality rather than a hospital.
* **Calm, breathing motion:** Use slow, intentional transitions and custom easing curves to lower cognitive anxiety and create a directed cinematic experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
* WCAG 2.1 AA compliant colors and typography.
* Full support for reduced-motion media query states.
* Screen reader navigation support.
